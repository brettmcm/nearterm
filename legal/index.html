Privacy Policy

Nearterm does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data services.

With your permission, Nearterm reads and updates reminders through Apple’s EventKit framework so it can show upcoming reminders and let you complete or reschedule them. Reminder information is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Nearterm’s widgets use an on-device snapshot stored in the app’s shared App Group. You can revoke Reminders access at any time in your device’s privacy settings.

Questions about this policy can be sent to brettmcm@me.com.
